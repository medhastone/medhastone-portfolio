HOW TO PLAY

🧠 Memorize: Tap cards to flip them and reveal the neon symbols underneath.

⚡ Match: Find two identical symbols to match them. They will be cleared from the board.

🔥 Combos: Match pairs quickly back-to-back (within 5 seconds) to build your combo multiplier and skyrocket your score.

🎯 Accuracy: Complete the level with as few wrong guesses as possible. Try to remember where symbols are so you don't flip the same wrong card twice!
