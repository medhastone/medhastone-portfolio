Privacy Policy

App: LexiBrain: Find Hidden Words (com.lexibrain.hiddenwords) • Effective Date: August 2026 • Developer: Medhastone

1 Introduction & Offline Privacy Commitment

Welcome to LexiBrain: Find Hidden Words (Package: com.lexibrain.hiddenwords), designed and published by Medhastone. We respect your privacy and are committed to maintaining a safe, transparent, and distraction-free educational word puzzle environment.

LexiBrain is built with an offline-first philosophy. We do not require account registration or social login. All puzzle solves, star ratings, coin balances, custom themes, and discovered Vocabulary Vault memory cards are stored locally on your device in an encrypted Room database with zero remote server tracking.

2 Data Collection & On-Device Storage

We categorize data into two explicit types:

A. Local Educational Game Progress (100% On-Device)

Your solved word levels, star rankings, streak statistics, unlocked board themes (Midnight, Classic, Cozy, Neon), and saved vocabulary flashcards are retained purely on your Android smartphone using SQLite/Room. Medhastone does not operate cloud database servers to harvest or monetize this information.

B. Advertising Diagnostics & Identifiers (Google AdMob)

To provide free access to all 500+ word levels without requiring mandatory paid subscriptions, LexiBrain integrates third-party mobile advertising via Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob may process non-personally identifying telemetry and advertising identifiers strictly in accordance with Google Play Developer Program policies.

3 Third-Party Advertising & Google AdMob Disclosures

LexiBrain integrates Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC) to serve banner advertisements, interstitial transitions, and opt-in rewarded video ads (e.g., watching a brief video to earn puzzle hints).

When advertising assets are loaded, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process technical information including:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID): A resettable advertising identifier used for ad frequency capping and attribution.
  • IP Address & Regional Telemetry: For regional ad serving, network routing, and fraud detection.
  • Device Model & OS Specifications: Screen dimensions, Android OS version, device manufacturer, and language preference.
  • Ad Engagement Metrics: Ad impressions, view duration, clicks, and dismissal actions.

Purpose: Ad data is utilized strictly for displaying advertisements, measuring campaign performance, and preventing fraudulent invalid traffic.

Google Policy Resources:

  • Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy)
  • How Google Uses Information from Sites/Apps (https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

User Opt-Out & Advertising ID Reset

You can reset your Google Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time through your Android device settings:
Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization (or Delete Advertising ID)

4 Device Permissions & Exact Usage

LexiBrain requests only the minimal technical permissions required for gameplay:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required exclusively by the Google AdMob SDK to request and render advertisements and rewarded videos. Gameplay itself functions 100% offline.
  • VIBRATE: Used locally for gentle haptic tactile feedback when selecting letters and completing words.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Prevents the screen from dimming while actively analyzing word search puzzle grids.

Note: LexiBrain does NOT request sensitive runtime permissions such as Camera, GPS Location, Microphone, Contacts, or Storage read/write.

5 Data Retention & User Deletion

In compliance with Google Play Data Safety standards:

  • Clear App Cache & Data: You can purge all local puzzle progress, vocabulary flashcards, and settings at any time via Android Settings > Apps > LexiBrain > Storage > Clear Storage.
  • App Uninstallation: Deleting the application permanently erases all associated on-device SQLite records and preferences.

6 Children's Privacy & COPPA Compliance

LexiBrain: Find Hidden Words is an educational family puzzle game suitable for audiences of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

AdMob integrations adhere to Google Play Families Policy requirements and respect COPPA treatment flags for non-personalized advertising where applicable.
