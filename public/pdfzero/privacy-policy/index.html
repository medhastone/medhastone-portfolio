Privacy Policy for PDFZero

Last Updated: March 2026 • Application: PDFZero: 100% Offline Universal PDF Toolkit (Package ID: com.pdfzero) • Developer: Medhastone

1. Core Commitment: 100% Offline Local Processing (Zero Cloud Uploads)

At Medhastone, user privacy is our foundational engineering principle. PDFZero is built as a zero-knowledge, strictly offline application. All document rendering, parsing, editing, merging, splitting, watermarking, redacting, compression, OCR extraction, and conversions occur 100% locally on your physical device.

We do NOT own, operate, or maintain any cloud document servers. Your bank statements, tax IDs, contracts, and personal records NEVER leave your phone or tablet.

2. Information Collection & Use

  • No Account Required: PDFZero requires no account creation, login, email address, or phone number.
  • No Document Telemetry: We do not log filenames, file sizes, document contents, or metadata.
  • No Personal Identification: We do not collect names, addresses, biometric identifiers, or contact details.

3. Device Permissions & Hardware Access

PDFZero requests only the minimum Android runtime permissions strictly necessary to execute user-initiated actions:

Storage / Media Access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / SAF)
Used solely to open your selected PDF files and save modified or converted documents back to your chosen storage directory via the standard Android Storage Access Framework.
Camera Access (CAMERA - Optional)
Used solely when you explicitly tap the "AI Document Scanner" or "ID Card Stitcher" tools to capture physical documents. Camera frames are processed in-memory and are never transmitted over the internet.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep PDFZero completely free and accessible without subscription paywalls, the application may display non-intrusive advertisements served by Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / AAID), IP address, and coarse device diagnostic information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising anytime in your Android device settings under Settings → Google → Ads.

Review Google's Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

PDFZero does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. All document processing remains strictly local to the device.

6. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

Because we do not upload or store any files on cloud infrastructure, there is no remote user data to retain or delete. You retain 100% control over all files saved in your device storage and can delete them at any time using your preferred file manager.
